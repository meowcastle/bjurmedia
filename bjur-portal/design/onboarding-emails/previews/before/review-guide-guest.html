Four steps
01 · Watch it through
Press play (or Space) and watch once before writing anything. Full screen is in the corner.
02 · Note on the frame
Pause where something should change and choose “Add a note at this moment”, or press N. The note is pinned to that second, so we see exactly what you saw. For anything about the whole cut, leave a General note.
03 · Send them together
Your notes are drafts while you write them: the panel says “Only you see these”. When you are done, press “Send notes to Bjur” (it counts them for you). They arrive as one batch, so we work from the full picture.
04 · Sign-off
Your project owner approves the final cut. Your notes go into the same round as theirs.
