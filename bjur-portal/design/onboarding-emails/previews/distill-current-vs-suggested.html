Kept distinctions

“Add a note at this moment” · “General note”Different things in the portal: a note pinned to a second vs. one about the whole cut.
“Approve” · “Sign-off”Only owners can approve (reviewAccess.canApprove). Everyone else is told the owner signs off.
login · personal linkA client seat signs in; a guest’s link is their only access and needs no password.
“Only you see these” · “Send notes to Bjur”A note is a private draft until it’s sent.
changed · kept as isThe two answers a note can get; the next cut’s email shows them.
login (noun) · sign in (verb)The account vs. the action. Same use as the existing welcome email.

2 · How reviews work, sent once

Current
How reviews work · Momentos - irl
Leaving notes on your cut takes a minute.

Hi Danny. Cut 1 of Sizzle reel — Oct 1st performance is waiting for you. Here is how the review screen works, so your notes land exactly where you mean them.

Sign in with your portal login and the cut opens in your project.

Four steps
01 · Watch it throughPress play (or Space) and watch once before writing anything. Full screen is in the corner.
02 · Note on the framePause where something should change and choose “Add a note at this moment”, or press N. The note is pinned to that second, so we see exactly what you saw. For anything about the whole cut, leave a General note.
03 · Send them togetherYour notes are drafts while you write them: the panel says “Only you see these”. When you are done, press “Send notes to Bjur” (it counts them for you). They arrive as one batch, so we work from the full picture.
04 · Or approve itNothing to change? Press “Approve cut 1” instead. That is how we know it is final.
Space play / pause · N note here · ← → one second · M next note
Open cut 1 →

Every note gets an answer. The next cut's email lists each one with what we did about it: changed, or kept as is and why.

The six laws

  1. One word. No retired phrasing in either template.
  2. Label-sufficient. No line repeats the headline, button or shortcut box.
  3. One fact, one place. Drafts are explained in the guide, the login in the cut email.
  4. Tier. No inbox, proxy or hashing words; “temporary password” only.
  5. Reversible. No destructive actions in either email.
  6. No lost meaning. All 9 review functions still named.
